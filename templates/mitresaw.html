MITRESaw runs on Shikari's own ATT&CK bundle (-f Enterprise -D -E -q -A -S), 20 groups at a time. Update collects only the groups an ATT&CK release changed; Full rebuild collects all of them (about 30 minutes). A stopped run carries on from its last finished batch. One rebuild runs at a time, and each is checked against Shikari's own walk of ATT&CK.

OSINT Repositories 

    
Community detection rules Shikari searches for each technique while generating: matches shape the query
      (OSINT Enhancement on the Hunt and Analyse tabs) and, with OSINT Comparison ticked, are scored against it. Cloned under
      data/repos on start-up and updated from upstream. LOLBAS, LOLDrivers and LOLRMM add indicators, with
      LOL datasets ticked (the default), only for the binaries, drivers and RMM tools a group is seen using; fetched at most daily. No AI is involved.
