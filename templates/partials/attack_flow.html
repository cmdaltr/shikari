About saved edits

Edits are kept when you are logged in and this group's results are in your library; otherwise they last until you leave the page.

  • Removing the group's last item from your library (or its Group/CVE tag) deletes the saved layout too; saving the group again starts from the default view.
  • A technique moved to another tactic is marked when that tactic isn't one ATT&CK gives it; its detections keep their own ATT&CK mapping (change that on the detection).
  • Freely placed cards (Option/Alt-drag) are offsets from their place in the lane. After regenerating a group, techniques that change tactic, or lanes that gain or lose techniques, can shift them.
  • The STIX download follows the SIEM filters, Hide Undetected, hidden techniques, removed and drawn links, and techniques moved to another tactic. Attack Flow has no layout, so free positions, orientation and order aren't exported, and the Links toggle only affects the display.
