Report branding Pro

Dress the three report products — the adversary-emulation assessment, the campaign-intelligence report and the detection/hunt report — in your own identity. Up to three logos lead the cover and the running header; your palette drives the chrome. Risk ratings keep their fixed colours so they stay readable, and Shikari keeps a co-brand line unless you turn it off. The setting is install-wide.
