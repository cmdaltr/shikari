Archer Hunting Application

Archer has been built to quickly build detection queries based on actionable intelligence attributed to a specific threat actor.

The app will search ATT&CK for documentated instances and obtain the respective TTPs. If the actor (or it's alias) does not exist in ATT&CK, you will be subsequently prompted for information to provide the TTPs the invoke.

Archer will produce detection queries for the respective TTPs for both MDE (KQL) and Splunk (SPL).
Archer compiles queries based on the data provided in the mappings and enriches simple searches with Generative AI to increase the fidelity.
